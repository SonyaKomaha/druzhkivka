FONTLOG for Druzhkivka Regular
==============================

This file provides information on the Druzhkivka Regular font software.
It should be distributed along with the font and any derivative works.


BASIC FONT INFORMATION
----------------------

Druzhkivka Regular is a display, all-caps sans-serif typeface with Latin and
Cyrillic support. It is inspired by Druzhkivka, the designer's hometown in
eastern Ukraine, and is a tribute to a place that can no longer be returned to.
Through its letterforms, the designer wanted to preserve the memory of the city.

The typeface is made for posters, signage, banners, social media graphics,
logos and apparel design, wherever typography needs to make a bold statement.
It comes in a single bold style and is not intended for long running text.

Design notes
- The design began with the Cyrillic capital Д, drawn intentionally as an
  expressive display character. It was recreated from memory of a distinctive
  capital seen in Druzhkivka, perhaps on a monument or a sign, and simplified
  to fit the typeface.
- The punctuation marks are deliberately thin, creating a contrast with the
  bold letterforms.
- The font is all-caps. Lowercase characters are included in the character map,
  but they use the same forms as the capitals, so typing in lowercase produces
  capital letters.

Character set
- 177 unique glyphs (294 glyph entries in the font file, because each lowercase
  character has its own copy of the capital form).
- Ukrainian alphabet (33 letters, including Ґ Є І Ї) and the apostrophe.
- Other Cyrillic letters: Ё Ы Э Ъ Ў Ј Љ Њ Ѓ Ѕ Ќ.
- Basic Latin A-Z and 44 additional Latin letters with diacritics and
  ligatures (for example Ą Ć Ę Ł Ń Ś Ź Ż Ö Ü Å Æ Œ Ø Ð Þ ẞ).
- Figures 0-9.
- Punctuation, quotation marks, dashes, brackets, mathematical signs and
  symbols (including № € @ & # % * and the ellipsis).
- Space and no-break space.

Language support (all letters of the standard alphabet are present)
- Cyrillic: Ukrainian, Belarusian, Bulgarian, Rusyn.
- Latin: Polish, English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian,
  Catalan, Galician, Occitan, Basque, Dutch, Danish, Norwegian, Swedish,
  Finnish, Icelandic, Faroese, Estonian, Albanian, Luxembourgish, Irish,
  Scottish Gaelic, Indonesian / Malay, Swahili, Zulu / Xhosa, Somali,
  Filipino, Latin.

Technical notes
- Format: OpenType with PostScript (CFF) outlines, 1000 units per em,
  cap height 700.
- Kerning is stored in the GPOS "kern" feature for the DFLT, cyrl and latn
  scripts. There is no legacy "kern" table. Some applications (for example
  video editors) may not apply GPOS kerning. In Microsoft Word, turn on
  "Kerning for fonts" in the font's advanced settings.
- Vertical metrics: ascender 950, descender -200, line gap 0.
- Designed in FontLab 8.

Known limitations
- Not fully supported: Serbian and Macedonian Cyrillic (missing Ђ Ћ Џ),
  Croatian, Bosnian and Slovenian (missing Č), Czech, Slovak, Hungarian,
  Romanian, Turkish, Latvian, Lithuanian, Welsh, Maltese and Esperanto.
- The hryvnia sign (₴) is not included.
